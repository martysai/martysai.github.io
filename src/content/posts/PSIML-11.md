In August, Serbia hosted [PSIML 11](https://psiml.pfe.rs/) — a summer school on machine learning for students from the country's top universities.

![PSIML 11 group photo](/assets/psiml-11-group-photo.jpg)

My personal highlights this year:

**1. The people are incredible.** Extremely smart students, many of whom I would want to work with full-time right now.

**2. We had five Russian-speaking lecturers** covering topics from audio to agents. It's great to see that my efforts to connect the Russian and Serbian ML communities are paying off.

**3. I made merch for the first time** — t-shirts, tote bags, thermoses, and even DIY mascot construction kits printed on 3D printers.

**4. Yandex became a sponsor of the school this year** and is even bringing us a rover to demo on sponsor day.

**5. I mentored a project on diffusion models in audio for watermarking.**

We even published the code on GitHub: [github.com/martysai/psiml11-audio-with-diffusion](https://github.com/martysai/psiml11-audio-with-diffusion)

And the models on HuggingFace: [AudioSeal robustness to diffusion attacks](https://huggingface.co/collections/msaidov/audioseal-robustness-to-diffusion-attacks)

![Lecturing at PSIML 11](/assets/psiml-11-lecture.jpg)

I was also glad to prepare and give a lecture on Automatic Speech Recognition.

PSIML is an important non-profit educational initiative for Serbia, and I'm very grateful that I get to do a lot of good things for it.
